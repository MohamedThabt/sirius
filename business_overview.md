# Project Sirius - Business Overview

## 🎯 The Core Concept
Sirius is an **AI-Powered Software Requirements & Project Orchestration Platform**. It serves as a highly intelligent digital product manager, designed to drastically reduce the time it takes to go from a raw software idea to actionable development blueprints. 

Instead of spending weeks writing documentation, brainstorming features, and designing databases, technical and non-technical founders or product teams can use Sirius to automatically generate these critical artifacts in minutes.

## 🏢 Business Function & Value Proposition
Software development often stalls in the planning phase. Sirius solves this by automating the heavy lifting of requirements engineering. By feeding the AI an initial idea, the platform generates everything a development team needs to start coding immediately.

### Key Offerings:
1. **Automated Documentation**: Instantly generates comprehensive Product Requirements Documents (PRDs) and System Overviews.
2. **Architecture & Visual Diagrams**: Uses AI to brainstorm and automatically draw foundational charts, including Entity Relationship Diagrams (ERD), C4 Architecture Models, Sequence Diagrams, and UI Wireframes.
3. **Module & Feature Breakdown**: Breaks down a macro-level software idea into bite-sized, logical "Modules" and actionable "Features."
4. **Technology Stack Recommendations**: Analyzes the project's goal and recommends the best technologies and frameworks (Technical Intake).
5. **Seamless Handoff to Developers**: Directly connects with GitHub to create repositories and push initial setups, bridging the gap between planning and execution.

## 🤝 Target Audience & Collaboration
- **Founders & Entrepreneurs**: Validate and structure their app ideas without needing deep technical knowledge.
- **Product Managers**: Easily draft PRDs and feature lists.
- **Development Teams & Agencies**: Skip the tedious setup phase and jump straight into coding.
- **Team Collaboration**: Users can invite team members to projects, assign roles, and work collaboratively via the team management and invitation system.

## 💳 Business Model
Sirius operates on a **SaaS (Software as a Service) Model**:
- **Subscription Plans**: Users subscribe to access the platform.
- **Credit-Based AI Usage**: Since generating AI documentation and diagrams relies on heavy computation, users consume "Credits" for AI requests. 
- **Beta Phase**: The project is currently equipped with a Beta Invitation and Feedback system, indicating an early-adopter rollout strategy to refine the product before a public launch.

## 🏁 Summary
At its highest level, Sirius acts as an "AI Co-Founder/Product Manager." It turns vague software concepts into fully structured, documented, and architected projects that are ready to be built, ultimately saving companies significant time and money during the project discovery phase.
