✅ VALID: `->>`  (solid synchronous message)
✅ VALID: `-->>`  (dotted asynchronous response)
✅ VALID: `->`   (solid line without arrowhead)
✅ VALID: `-->`  (dotted line without arrowhead)
✅ VALID: `-x`   (solid line with cross at end)
✅ VALID: `--x`  (dotted line with cross at end)
❌ NEVER use: `SOLID_OPEN_ARROW`, `DOTTED_POINT`, or any other invented syntax
